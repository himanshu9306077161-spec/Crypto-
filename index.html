How It Works

GitHub Actions runs every day at 9 AM IST, fetches live prices and 5-year history for 200 top coins from CoinGecko, runs the Alpha Beta Gamma algorithm, and saves results. The website reads this data instantly when you open it.

α β γ Formula
Final Score = α(40%) + β(15%) + γ(25%) + Momentum(20%) α Alpha = Coin outperformance vs Bitcoin β Beta = Market sensitivity (ideal: 1.2x–3.0x) γ Gamma = Momentum acceleration (speeding up?) Momentum = Multi-timeframe trend strength

Works in BOTH bull and bear markets. All scores normalized to 0–100 relative to current market conditions.

Future Price Predictions

Predictions use linear regression on historical momentum + seasonal patterns. Each coin card shows 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day price targets based on current trajectory. These are mathematical projections — not guaranteed.
