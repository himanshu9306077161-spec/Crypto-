Signal Rules

⭐ STRONG BUY — Best momentum today + strong multi-year history.

🟢 BUY — Score ≥ 65 and positive 24h move. Good entry today.

Score Formula

Trend Score = 24h change (25%) + 7d change (35%) + 30d change (40%), normalized to 0–100. Historical signal adds up to +10 bonus for coins with strong multi-year track record.
