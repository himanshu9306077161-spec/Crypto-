Alpha β Gamma — How Signals Are Calculated
Score = Alpha(40%) + Beta(25%) + Gamma(35%) Alpha = Coin 30d return − BTC 30d return Beta = Coin 30d move ÷ BTC 30d move Gamma = (Coin 7d − Monthly avg) ÷ Monthly avg

This is a professional quant model used by hedge funds. It filters out coins that only rose because Bitcoin rose, and finds coins with genuine independent strength.

α Alpha — Outperformance

Alpha measures how much a coin outperforms Bitcoin. If BTC went up 10% in 30 days and your coin went up 25%, the Alpha is +15%. Positive Alpha means the coin has its own strength — not just riding Bitcoin's wave. Weight: 40% of total score.

β Beta — Market Sensitivity

Beta tells you how much a coin moves relative to Bitcoin. Beta of 2.0 means when BTC goes up 10%, this coin goes up 20%. For 8% profit target, the sweet spot is Beta between 1.2 and 3.0 — enough movement to hit target quickly, not so volatile it crashes. Weight: 25% of total score.

γ Gamma — Momentum Acceleration

Gamma detects if momentum is speeding up or slowing down. If a coin gained 5% per week on average this month but gained 15% this week, Gamma is very positive — acceleration is happening, likely to continue. If it slowed from 15% to 5%, Gamma is negative — momentum dying. Weight: 35% of total score.
