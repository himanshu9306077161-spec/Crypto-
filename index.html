How Data Is Collected

Every day at 9 AM IST GitHub automatically runs a script that:

  • Fetches live prices from Binance — same source as your exchange
  • Downloads 5 years of daily history per coin from CoinGecko
  • Calculates yearly returns, monthly patterns, support/resistance, volatility
  • Saves everything to a file your phone reads instantly
How to Read the Cards

Tap any coin card to expand it and see full historical analysis. The circle shows the Trend Score (0–100). The ⭐ badge means the coin has a strong long-term history (2+ years, avg return > 30%).

Signal Explanation

🟢 BUY — Score ≥ 65 + positive 24h. Good today.

⭐ STRONG BUY — BUY signal + historically strong coin (years of data confirm it).

🟡 WATCH — Score 40–64. Not ready yet.

🔴 AVOID — Weak momentum today.

Monthly Seasonality

Each coin card shows a bar chart of its average return by month over the past 5 years. Green bars = historically good months to buy. The current month is highlighted with a blue outline. If you are in a green month, that coin has historically performed well right now.
